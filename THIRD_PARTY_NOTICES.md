redistribution of the entire codebase and all game assets. The above tool
notices apply to their respective components, **not** automatically to
Pokémon characters, game assets, inherited sound or every source file.

by themselves establish permission to redistribute third-party content.

The README's original AI-generated atlas banner is concept artwork,
**not an in-game screenshot**. Pokémon names, characters and trademarks
remain with their respective owners. No endorsement by Nintendo,
Game Freak, The Pokémon Company, or the upstream projects is implied.

**This repository is source-only:** it must not distribute compiled GBA ROM
images, game dumps, personal saves or private development Git history.
Issues and contributions should respect original credit and component
permission requirements. Source visibility is not a claim that the game is
finished, playable at every commit, or verified to contain all 1,025
catchable Pokémon.
